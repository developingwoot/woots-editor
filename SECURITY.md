# Security

Woots Editor runs on your own computer. It listens on `127.0.0.1` only (nothing else on your network can reach it), has no accounts and no login of its own, and
refuses requests that do not come from its own page. Claude runs it starts are limited to their own folder and cannot run programs other than the few they are allowed.
The fact check is the one step that searches the web.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" on the Security tab of this repository (private), or open an issue if it is not sensitive.
Please say which version (shown at the foot of Settings) and what you did.
