# The channel's style

- `house-style.md`: tone and habits that apply to every video (also written from the Tone, Every video and No video fields in Settings).
- `brand.md`: colours and fonts (written from the Brand fields in Settings).
- `director-notes.md`: optional. Your own notes on how you like your videos made; every background run reads it when it exists.

Files you edit by hand are never overwritten by the app.
