# Cut rules

## Never ship a double cut
Two visible picture changes within about a second look like a mistake. Check every preview for pairs of cuts closer than 1.5 s (angle changes, punch-in starts and ends, full-frame B-roll edges) and merge or move one.
Common causes: a punch-in that starts a few frames after the cut instead of on it; a block edge a few frames off a camera-angle change; B-roll anchored on the exact block boundary instead of a frame inside it.

## Slow moves and callouts
- A zoom or pan is slow enough to be felt, not seen. If it is noticed, slow it down.
- A callout (arrow, circle, label) lands on the word it points at and leaves before the next idea.

## Judgment
- Cut to serve the viewer who knows nothing: if a term is used before it is explained, either move the explanation or add it.
- A joke or aside that makes the creator sound like themselves stays, even when it is not strictly needed.
- When unsure whether to cut, keep it and flag it.
