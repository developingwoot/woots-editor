# Editing style

- **Do not over-tighten.** A video with no breaths feels like jump, jump, jump. Cut dead air, keep the pauses that let a point land.
- **Trim weak passages, do not delete them.** Remove repetition and waffle; keep the question, the concrete facts and first-hand experience.
- A tangent that interrupts a verdict or conclusion is fair to cut.
- Every editorial cut carries a written reason, so the creator can veto it.
- Keep the face on screen when you can. Break a long cutaway by returning to the face during lines that need no picture.
- **A visual change every 15-30 seconds**: a camera angle, B-roll of the thing discussed, a graphic, a screenshot, a punch-in. Do not force it; each change is motivated by what is said. If nothing fits, leave it and say so.
