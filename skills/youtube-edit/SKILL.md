# Editing a talking-head video

Start with `rough-cut.md`; the numbers it names live in `rough-cut.json`.

- `rough-cut.md`: how the cut is built from recordings (order, pauses, pieces, beats, repeats, cutaways, punch-ins, the opening). The rules the app checks a cut against.
- `rough-cut.json`: every number those rules use. Change a number here and the next cut follows it.
- `cut-rules.md`: finer rules about what to cut and keep.
- `editing-style.md`: the feel of the edit: rhythm, energy, what a viewer notices.
- `story.md`: how to find the story in what was filmed: the question, the opening, the setback, the payoff, what is weak, which unplanned moments to keep.
- `takes-and-splicing.md`: choosing between takes of the same line and joining pieces.
- `lessons.md`: dated lines taught by the host's notes.
- `references.json`: the measurements of reference videos used by the scorecard.
