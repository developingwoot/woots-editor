# Shorts

- `shorts.md`: the rules for choosing a moment: a complete thought, a hook in the first seconds, a payoff, no need for the rest of the video to understand it.
- `shorts.json`: the numbers: shortest and longest length, how many per video, the score a moment must reach, caption size and style, the hook time, sound-effect spacing.

A short stands alone. If it needs the video to make sense, it is not a short yet.
