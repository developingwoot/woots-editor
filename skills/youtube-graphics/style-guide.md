# Graphic style

- One idea per graphic. Three words beat a sentence.
- Type is large enough to read on a phone: headline at least 7% of the frame height.
- Use the brand colours and fonts from `woots-channel/brand.md`; at most three colours in one graphic.
- Leave the face clear. Lower third and side panels for anything that sits over the speaker.
- Enter in 0.3-0.5 s, hold long enough to read it twice, leave in 0.3 s.
- Every graphic is timed to the word it illustrates (see the word times given with the beat).
- No lower-thirds with the host's name, no logos in the corner unless the plan asks for them.
