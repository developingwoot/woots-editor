# Motion

- Ease out when something arrives, ease in when it leaves. Nothing moves at constant speed.
- Stagger a group by 0.06-0.12 s so the eye is led through it.
- Use depth sparingly: a slight camera push (3-6%) over the length of a graphic keeps it alive.
- Numbers count up; bars grow from their baseline; a marker lands with a small overshoot.
- A kinetic caption highlights the word as it is said, not before.
- Never animate two things for attention at once.
