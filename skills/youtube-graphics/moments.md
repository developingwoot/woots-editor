# Fun moments

A fun moment is a very short pop-up that makes a good line land: a sticker on a joke, a number that jumps, a burst on a reveal, a few words on a punchline. It is not an explanation (teaching moments are for that).

- Pick lines that are already funny, surprising or a result. Never decorate a flat line.
- One word to four words of on-screen text, in the host's own voice.
- Kinds: `stamp` (a sticker with a few words), `burst` (a short burst of shapes), `counter` (a number that counts to a result), `kinetic` (a few words that move).
- Never on a beat that already has a graphic or a teaching moment.
- Few and short: the numbers are in `graphics.json`. When in doubt, leave it out.
