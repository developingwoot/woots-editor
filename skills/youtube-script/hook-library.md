# Opening patterns

Written for this app, not copied from any channel. Pick one that fits the video's real content; never promise what the video does not deliver.

1. **The result first.** Show the finished thing (or the failure) in the first five seconds, then: "here is how I got there."
2. **The question.** Ask the question the viewer already has, in their words, and say the video will answer it.
3. **The wrong belief.** State what most people think, then say what you found instead.
4. **The stakes.** Say what it cost, how long it took or what nearly went wrong, then start the story.
5. **The challenge.** A clear test with a rule and a deadline ("one afternoon, no spare parts").
6. **The comparison.** Two options, one question, a promise to settle it with evidence.
7. **The confession.** A mistake made on camera, and what it taught.

## Test of a hook
- Could a stranger say what the video is about after ten seconds?
- Is there something to see, not only something said?
- Does the video pay it off within the first third?
