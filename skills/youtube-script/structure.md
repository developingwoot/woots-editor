# Structure of a video

- **Logline**: one sentence: who wants what and what stands in the way.
- **Thesis**: what the video concludes.
- **Promise**: what the viewer gets by staying.

## Shape
1. **Hook (0-30 s)**: the question, the problem or the result, shown before it is explained.
2. **Context (short)**: only what the viewer needs to care. Cut anything they can learn later.
3. **Middle**: a chain of "so, but" steps, each ending on a question the next step answers. Alternate explanation with doing.
4. **Setback**: something that does not go to plan, and what was learned.
5. **Verdict**: the answer, with the evidence in the order the viewer will believe it.
6. **Ending**: one next step (another video, a download, a try-it-yourself). No recap of the whole video.

## Pacing
- A new idea or a new picture every 15-30 seconds.
- Keep sections between 45 seconds and 3 minutes; split anything longer with a visual change.
- Open loops (a question raised, answered later) carry attention across sections.
