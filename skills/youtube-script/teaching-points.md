# Teaching points

A teaching point is a term or idea the viewer needs before the next step makes sense.

- Decide who the viewer is (see the channel profile's audience). Anything they would have to look up is a teaching point.
- Explain it once, at the moment it first matters, in one or two sentences and one picture. Not before: an explanation with nothing to apply it to is forgotten.
- Say what it does for them, then what it is called.
- Use an analogy only if it is accurate for the part you are using.
- Pair every teaching point with a graphic or a shot, and note it in the outline as `[NOTE: TP01 | line S08 | term: ... | VO (Host): "..."]`.
- Do not explain what the audience already knows; it reads as talking down.
