# Writing the outline

An outline is a list of sections. Each part has a `[VISUAL: ...]` line (what is on screen), then the host speaking (`HOST (A-ROLL):` to camera, `HOST (VO):` voice-over) with suggested wording in quotes. The host speaks in their own words; the wording is a loose guide.

Read `structure.md` for the shape of a video, `hook-library.md` for opening patterns and `teaching-points.md` for explaining terms.

Check an outline against this list before handing it back:
1. The hook makes a promise in the first 15 seconds and the video keeps it.
2. Every section has at least one `[VISUAL]` line.
3. A term a first-time viewer does not know is explained once, where it first matters.
4. There is a setback or a surprise in the middle: a video with no friction is a manual.
5. The ending answers the opening question and says what to do next.
